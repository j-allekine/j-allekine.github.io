How I work

Build, prove, hand off.

I do not build and disappear. I map the messy parts, clarify what matters, wire up the automation, and test the system against real work before handoff.

My approach connects operations, data, and software. The tool can be Excel, Google Sheets, Apps Script, APIs, or a custom application. The goal stays the same: less friction, clearer decisions, and a system people can maintain.

I treat time, data, and technology as resources to steward well. The goal is not automation for its own sake, but work that genuinely serves the people using it.

01

Map

I study the current workflow, people, handoffs, bottlenecks, and exceptions.

02

Build

I design and automate the smallest dependable system that solves the real problem.

03

Prove

I test the workflow, validate the data, and make sure the numbers actually move.

04

Hand off

I document, train, and hand over a system that can keep running without me.
