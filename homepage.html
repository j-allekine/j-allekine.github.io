How I work

Discover, build, launch.

I do not build and disappear. I map the messy parts, clarify what matters, build around the real workflow, and launch only after the system has been tested against real work.

My approach connects operations, data, and software. The tool can be Excel, Google Sheets, Apps Script, APIs, or a custom application. The goal stays the same: less friction, clearer decisions, and a system people can maintain.

I treat time, data, and technology as resources to steward well. The goal is not automation for its own sake, but work that genuinely serves the people using it.

01

Discovery

We start with a focused call to understand your current process, pain points, tools involved, and the outcome you want to achieve.

02

Plan

I turn your process into a clear roadmap — what happens, who's responsible, what to do when things don't go as expected. You'll see the whole system before I build a single line, so there are no surprises later.

03

Build

Built around how you actually work, not a generic template. Tested on real scenarios, refined with your feedback, so what launches is something your team already trusts.

04

Launch

You're not left to figure it out alone. I hand it over with clear documentation and hands-on support, so the transition feels steady, not stressful.
