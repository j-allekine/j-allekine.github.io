# 04 — Understand capabilities and make contact

**What to build:** Complete the homepage narrative and shared follow-up path. A hiring manager or potential client can understand the operational problems J. Allekine can handle, review featured work, and find honest email, resume, and GitHub actions after reading either the homepage or a case study.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Open one featured case study.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The homepage follows the agreed content sequence: text-led hero, featured work, “What I do,” contact call to action, and minimal footer.
- [ ] The hero supports a role label, replaceable positioning statement, supporting introduction, work anchor, and optional availability or location metadata without using a portrait.
- [ ] “What I do” is a structured capability ledger focused on operational problems and practical improvement, not a tool list, logo cloud, skill-card grid, or proficiency display.
- [ ] The contact call to action is the strongest black section on the page, with direct email as the primary action and resume and GitHub as secondary actions.
- [ ] Email, resume, and GitHub slots remain visible when unconfigured, are clearly marked as provisional, and do not render as anchors with fake addresses, placeholder URLs, broken destinations, or `#` links.
- [ ] No contact form, submission service, backend, or additional contact page is introduced.
- [ ] The shared contact call to action appears after the main content on the homepage and every case study.
- [ ] The visually quiet footer supports configurable identity text, optional location, the current copyright year, and a working back-to-top link without repeating a large navigation menu.
- [ ] Homepage and contact language position J. Allekine as an operational problem-solver, keep automation as a method rather than the entire identity, and avoid unsupported claims or developer jargon.
- [ ] Browser acceptance coverage verifies homepage section order, the shared contact and footer placement, back-to-top behavior, and configured versus unconfigured external-action behavior.
- [ ] The production build, browser acceptance suite, and repository diff hygiene check pass.
