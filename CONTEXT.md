# Professional Portfolio

This portfolio presents J. Allekine's ability to understand operational problems and build practical automated systems.

## Language

**Operational problem-solver**:
The portfolio's core professional identity: someone who understands inefficient or unreliable work and improves it through practical systems.
_Avoid_: Generalist, tool expert, automation developer

**Automation**:
A method used to improve operations by reducing repetitive work, preventable errors, and fragile handoffs. It is not the portfolio's entire professional identity.
_Avoid_: The product, the identity

**Faith signal**:
A restrained visual expression of J. Allekine's Christian faith that attentive visitors can discover without it becoming the portfolio's primary message.
_Avoid_: Church branding, religious stock imagery, explicit faith statement

**Case study**:
A dedicated account of one project that demonstrates the operational problem, J. Allekine's decisions and contribution, the resulting system, and credible evidence of improvement.
_Avoid_: Project card, tool showcase, image gallery

**Featured work**:
The three case studies selected for direct access from the homepage.
_Avoid_: All projects, project gallery

**Primary audience**:
Hiring managers assessing J. Allekine for roles involving workflow automation, operational systems, spreadsheets, or data.
_Avoid_: Everyone, general audience

**Secondary audience**:
Potential clients exploring whether J. Allekine can improve a manual or unreliable business workflow.
_Avoid_: Primary audience

**Guiding content**:
Clearly provisional copy or media that demonstrates a section's intended purpose, hierarchy, and approximate content shape until J. Allekine supplies approved content.
_Avoid_: Final copy, invented personal claims
